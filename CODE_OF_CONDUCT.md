# Code of Conduct

Starfire Engine contributors and maintainers agree to keep project spaces respectful, welcoming, and focused on constructive technical collaboration.

Harassment, threats, targeted abuse, discriminatory comments, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's private security/advisory mechanisms or the organization's published contact channel. Maintainers will review reports, handle them confidentially where possible, and may remove content or restrict participation.
